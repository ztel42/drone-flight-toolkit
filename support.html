Drone Flight Toolkit Support

Drone Flight Toolkit is a pre-flight helper for drone pilots, available for Android and iPhone.

Contact

Questions, problems, or feedback? Email solinvicta4252@gmail.com.

Manage or cancel Pro

Android (Google Play)

Open the Play Store, then go to Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions and select Drone Flight Toolkit.

iPhone

Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions and select Drone Flight Toolkit.

Restore Purchases

To restore Pro on a new or reinstalled device, open the app and tap Restore Purchases.
