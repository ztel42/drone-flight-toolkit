This privacy policy explains what information Drone Flight Toolkit (the "app") accesses, where that information goes, and the choices you have. 1. Summary

  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app shows no advertising, has no sign-in and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (software development kits). It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The developer does not run any servers, and the app sends nothing to the developer. The one exception is limited transaction records that Google Play or Apple provides to the developer if you buy Pro (Section 4.4).
  • Android only: Google's billing library sends Google diagnostics. The Android app includes one Google SDK, the Play Billing Library, which is needed to offer Pro through Google Play. That library sends Google limited technical diagnostics about its own billing operations, such as whether a billing request succeeded, along with basic device information. This happens whenever the app checks your Pro status, even if you never buy Pro (Section 3.2.1). The Android app also includes the open-source AboutLibraries library, which only displays the license notices built into the app. It works offline, makes no network connections, and collects no data.
  • iPhone: no billing SDK and no diagnostics. The iPhone app uses StoreKit, which is part of iOS, to offer Pro through Apple's App Store. It contains no third-party SDKs or libraries, and the app's own code sends no diagnostics to anyone (Section 3.2.2).
  • Location is optional and approximate. The app asks for approximate location only when you tap Use my approximate location. It never asks for precise or background location.
  • Some data goes to U.S. government weather services. When you run a Go/No-Go check (Check conditions), the app requests public weather data from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). If you entered or located a site position, that request includes a search area around that position, rounded to about 0.1° (roughly 11 km) first, so the exact position is not sent (see Section 3).
  • Pro (optional): the core app is free. That includes Go/No-Go safety checks, the default five-item pre-flight checklist, saving, sharing and deleting your logs, and viewing SRT flights. Pro is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription (US$2.99/month in the U.S.), sold through Google Play on Android and through the App Store on iPhone. It adds GPX/KML/ASS telemetry export, CSV export of all checklist logs, aircraft/site quick-fill, and custom checklists. Google or Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or bank details (Section 4).
  • Everything else stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it, or your device's own backup includes it (Section 3.5).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

2. Information the app accesses and how it is used

2.1 Approximate device location (optional)

  • When: only after you tap Use my approximate location and grant location permission. The app does not request location at start-up, does not request precise location and does not access location in the background.
    • Android: the app asks for approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only, never precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION). Android limits approximate location to roughly a 3 square kilometer area.
    • iPhone: the app asks for location access While Using the App only, never "Always". iOS offers approximate location by default for this app (Precise Location off), and the app itself requests only reduced accuracy. Even if you turn Precise Location on in Settings, the app still rounds the position before any weather request (below).
  • What: a single approximate position.
  • How it is used: the position fills in the latitude and longitude fields on the Go/No-Go screen. When you run a check, the app uses the position as is, on your device, to compute sunrise, sunset and twilight times and to pick nearby weather stations. For the weather request to aviationweather.gov, the app first rounds it to the nearest 0.1° and sends only a search area around the rounded point (Section 3).
  • Storage: the position is kept only in the app's memory while the app is running. The app does not save it to storage.

If you prefer not to share your device location, deny the permission. You can still type an airport station code (ICAO) or coordinates yourself.

2.2 Information you type in

  • Go/No-Go: an ICAO weather station code (for example, KXMR), optional site latitude and longitude, and a "night = don't fly" setting. Coordinates you type are handled exactly like approximate location in Section 2.1: used unrounded on your device, and rounded to the nearest 0.1° before a search area around them is sent to aviationweather.gov when you run a check.
  • Checklist: item check marks, aircraft, site and notes, plus, with Pro, the items you write for a custom checklist. Anything you type in these fields stays on your device (on iPhone, some of it can be included in your own device backup; see Section 3.5.2).

2.3 Flight telemetry files you open

When you open a DJI .SRT flight log, you pick the file through the system file picker (Android: the system file picker; iPhone: the Files document picker). The app can read only the file you choose and has no general storage or photo-library permission. The file's contents, which can include the drone's GPS track, altitude, speed and timestamps, are processed in memory on your device. The app does not send the file anywhere. Viewing a flight is free. GPX, KML or ASS exports (Pro) are written only to a location you choose or sent to an app you pick from the share sheet.

2.5 What the app does not collect

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, SMS or call logs, Bluetooth, or health data, and does not ask for your name or email address. It never sees your payment card or bank details. It does not read your advertising ID (Android advertising ID or iPhone IDFA) or any other device identifier, and it does not ask for permission to track you (the iPhone app has no App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does no tracking). The app's own code does not gather analytics, usage statistics or crash reports. On Android, the only diagnostics that leave the app are the Google Play Billing Library's own diagnostics, sent to Google (Section 3.2.1). On iPhone, the app sends no diagnostics. Separately from the app, Google and Apple may give the developer aggregated statistics and, if you agreed in your device settings, crash reports (Section 3.3).

3. Who receives data from the app

3.1 NOAA weather services (when you run a Go/No-Go check)

The app connects directly from your device to these public U.S. government services over encrypted HTTPS:

Service Operated by What the app sends Purpose
https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/metar NOAA National Weather Service, Aviation Weather Center The ICAO station codes you entered or that the app chose near your site. If a site position was entered or located, the app also sends a rectangular search area of ±0.75° latitude/longitude (about 80 km) around that position, after rounding the position to the nearest 0.1°. To get recent METAR aviation weather reports
https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/json/planetary_k_index_1m.json NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center No information you entered To get the current planetary Kp (geomagnetic) index

The search area is centered on the rounded position, so the service could work out roughly where your site is (to within about 0.1° of latitude and longitude, about 11 km or less) but not the exact coordinates. The unrounded coordinates are never sent. The station codes the app picks near your site also indicate the general area.

As with any internet request, these services also receive your device's IP address, the time of the request, and the app's identifier (User-Agent): DroneFlightToolkit/1.0 (Android; +https://github.com/ztel42/PythonCollection) on Android, or DroneFlightToolkit/1.0 (iOS; +https://github.com/ztel42/PythonCollection) on iPhone. The app does not send any account, name, device ID or advertising ID. The iPhone app uses a connection that stores no cookies and keeps no response cache on disk.

These services are not operated by the developer, and their own privacy policies apply. The National Weather Service states that it logs details of each web request (including the IP address and the item requested) and may keep those logs. See:

  • National Weather Service privacy policy: https://www.weather.gov/privacy
  • NOAA privacy policy: https://www.noaa.gov/protecting-your-privacy

The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA, the National Weather Service or the FAA.

3.2 App store billing

3.2.1 Android: Google Play billing (Google)

The Android app uses Google's Play Billing Library to offer Pro. Google operates Google Play and this library. Data reaches Google in two ways:

  1. Through the Google Play app on your device. Each time you open or return to the app, it asks Google Play whether you have Pro. It also does this when you view or buy Pro, tap Restore purchases, or confirm (acknowledge) a new purchase, as Google requires. Google Play then contacts Google's servers under Google's own terms and privacy policy. If there is a problem with a renewal payment, the app may also let Google Play show its own message asking you to fix your payment method. Purchases themselves are handled by Google Play (Section 4).
  2. Directly from the billing library: diagnostics. The billing library sends Google technical logs about its own operation, over encrypted HTTPS. Google says these logs give "insight into API usage (such as success and failure) and service connection issues" and that the information is used "to improve the performance of the Play Billing Library and provide better support for errors."
    • What is sent: Google does not publish an exact list. Based on our review of the library, the logs record which billing functions ran and their results. Google's logging component adds basic device and app details:
      • device model and manufacturer;
      • Android version and build;
      • language and region settings;
      • mobile network operator code and connection type;
      • time zone offset;
      • the app's version.
    • What is not sent: we did not find your name, email address, advertising ID, location, or payment details in these logs.
    • When: because the app checks your Pro status whenever you open it, these diagnostics are sent even if you never view or buy Pro.
    • Storage on your device: messages waiting to be sent are held briefly in the app's private storage.
    • Who gets them: the logs go to Google, not to the developer. We cannot access or delete them. Google handles them under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The Android app's own code sends nothing to Google beyond the billing requests described above.

3.2.2 iPhone: Apple App Store (StoreKit)

The iPhone app offers Pro through Apple's in-app purchase system using StoreKit, which is part of iOS. The app contains no billing SDK and no other third-party code, and it sends no diagnostics.

  • Each time you open or return to the app, it asks StoreKit on your device whether you have Pro. It also does this when you view or buy Pro, tap Restore Purchases, or open Manage Subscription. StoreKit, not the app, contacts Apple's servers when needed, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple handles the purchase itself, including your Apple Account, payment method and purchase history (Section 4).
  • Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3.3 Statistics from Google Play and Apple

As with any app in their stores, Google Play and Apple give the developer statistics about the app through the Google Play Console and App Store Connect, such as numbers of installs, updates, sales and countries. These are generally aggregated and do not identify you. If you have chosen in your device settings to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers (Android: Google's usage and diagnostics setting; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share With App Developers), Google or Apple may also give the developer crash and performance reports for the app. These come from Google or Apple under their own policies, not from the app, and you can turn that sharing off in the same settings. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

3.4 Apps and places you choose

Data leaves the app in only two other ways, and only when you choose to:

  • Share: the system share sheet appears, and the data goes to the app you pick, such as email or a messaging app (on iPhone, also AirDrop or Files). That app's privacy policy then applies. This happens when you:
    • share a checklist log;
    • use Share report on a Go/No-Go result (the report can include your site coordinates as entered, not rounded);
    • share a telemetry export (Pro); or
    • share the CSV file of all your checklist logs (Pro). The CSV includes every saved log's times, aircraft, site, notes and item states, including any custom checklist item names.
  • Export: when you save a GPX, KML or ASS file, or the CSV of all logs (Pro), it is written to the location you choose.

3.5 Backups and moving to a new device

Backups and device transfers are run by Google or Apple, not by the developer, and the data in them never goes to the developer. The two platforms work differently.

3.5.1 Android

The Android app excludes its data from Android cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, if you use Android's device-to-device transfer while setting up a new phone, Android may copy your saved checklist logs, your Pro custom checklist (custom_checklist.json, which is stored with the logs), and app settings (checklist in progress, including custom-item check marks, last ICAO code, night setting) to the new device. The custom checklist is copied even if Pro isn't active. It is used only while Pro is active on the new device. You can delete it there at any time, with or without Pro (Section 6). Logs you have already deleted are not included. Location is never included because it is not saved. The Pro status file is never included in a backup or transfer; it is kept in a part of the app's storage that Android excludes, and the app's backup rules also exclude it. Your Pro subscription follows your Google account instead (Section 4.6).

3.5.2 iPhone

  • Excluded from backups: the app marks its checklist_logs folder (your saved checklist logs and your Pro custom checklist) and its Pro status file as excluded from backup. They are not included in iCloud Backup or in backups to a computer. iOS has no separate "transfer only" setting, so these files may not move to a new iPhone either. To keep your logs, share or save them (for example to Files) before you change phones.
  • Included in your device backup: the app's small settings (the checklist in progress, including custom-item check marks, aircraft, site and notes; the last ICAO code; and the night setting) are stored in the app's UserDefaults, like most apps' preferences. iOS includes them in your device backups: in iCloud Backup, if you have turned it on, and in backups you make to a computer. iCloud Backups are stored by Apple under your Apple Account and encrypted by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud or computer backups.
  • Never backed up: temporary share copies (they are in the app's temporary folder) and your location (it is not saved).
  • Your Pro subscription follows your Apple Account instead (Section 4.6).

3.6 No ads, no sale

The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. If we are ever legally required to provide information, the only information we would have is what we hold directly: emails you send us, the statistics and reports described in Section 3.3, and, if you bought Pro, the transaction records described in Section 4.4.

4. Purchases and the Pro subscription (Pro)

4.1 Free features and Pro

Free for everyone: the Go/No-Go weather and safety check, the default five-item pre-flight checklist, saving, sharing and deleting individual checklist logs, deleting a saved custom checklist, and opening and viewing DJI SRT flights (track, HUD and stats).

Drone Flight Toolkit Pro is an optional, auto-renewing monthly subscription. It adds:

  • saving and sharing flight tracks as GPX, KML and ASS (HUD subtitle) files;
  • exporting all saved checklist logs as one CSV spreadsheet;
  • one-tap aircraft and site quick-fill, using the aircraft and site names in your own saved logs (this happens on your device); and
  • custom checklists: you can add, edit, reorder and remove items, or start from the Default, Recreational or Part 107 preset. Your custom checklist is stored only on your device. Presets are reminders only, not legal advice; see the Terms of Use.

The price, billing period and renewal terms are shown in the app and on Google Play's or the App Store's purchase screen before you confirm. The U.S. price is US$2.99 per month. Prices in other countries are set in local currency and may include taxes. There is no free trial. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. A subscription bought on Android does not unlock Pro on iPhone, or the other way around, because Google and Apple sell them separately.

  • iPhone: purchases are made with your Apple Account through the App Store. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Apple collects and processes your payment method, billing details and purchase records under its own terms and privacy policy:

4.3 What the app receives and keeps on your device

Android. When you buy Pro, and each time the app checks your subscription, Google Play gives the app, on your device, the purchase record for this app's subscription. The record includes details such as the order ID and purchase time. The app uses only:

  • the product ID;
  • the purchase state (purchased or pending);
  • whether the subscription is on hold or paused;
  • whether it is set to auto-renew;
  • whether the purchase has been acknowledged; and
  • for a new purchase, the purchase token, which identifies the purchase.

It uses these, in memory, only to:

  • unlock or lock Pro features (Pro is unlocked only after Google Play confirms payment, never while a payment is pending);
  • confirm (acknowledge) a new purchase back to Google Play, which Google requires; and
  • show your subscription status and the Manage subscription option.

iPhone. When you buy Pro, and each time the app checks your subscription, StoreKit gives the app, on your device, Apple-signed transaction and subscription information for this app's subscription. The app accepts only information that passes StoreKit's signature check. It uses only:

  • the product ID;
  • whether the transaction passed verification;
  • whether you bought it or it is shared through Family Sharing (Pro is not available through Family Sharing);
  • the expiration date, and any revocation (for example, after a refund);
  • the subscription's renewal state (subscribed, in a billing grace period, in billing retry, expired or revoked) and whether it will auto-renew.

It uses these, in memory, only to unlock or lock Pro features (never while a purchase is pending, for example awaiting Ask to Buy approval), to complete ("finish") verified transactions as StoreKit requires, and to show your subscription status and the Manage Subscription option.

What the app saves (both platforms): only two values, whether Pro is active and when Google Play or the App Store last confirmed it. They are kept in a small file in the app's private storage (Android: no_backup/pro_status.properties; iPhone: pro_status.json). The file is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer on Android, and from iCloud and computer backups on iPhone. The app does not save the purchase token, transaction ID, order ID, receipt or any payment details.

Offline use: the app re-checks every time you open or return to it. If Google Play or the App Store can't be reached, the app keeps Pro unlocked only if your subscription was confirmed within the last 3 days. After that, Pro features stay locked until your subscription is confirmed again. Free features always keep working.

The app does not send purchase details to the developer or to anyone other than Google Play or Apple. The developer does not run a server that receives or verifies purchases; your subscription is checked on your device with Google Play or StoreKit.

4.4 What the developer receives from Google or Apple

As the seller, the developer receives transaction records:

  • Android, through the Google Play Console. Depending on Google's reports, these can include:
    • the order number, the product, and the price and tax;
    • the date and the status (for example, charged, refunded or canceled);
    • the buyer's country and, where Google provides them, the buyer's state or province, city and postal code.
  • iPhone, through App Store Connect. Apple's sales, subscription and financial reports show the product, units, prices, proceeds, dates, subscription events (for example, renewals, cancellations and refunds) and the country or region of sale. They generally do not identify individual customers, and Apple does not give us your name, email address or Apple Account.

Neither Google nor Apple gives us your payment card details. We use these records only for accounting, tax, refunds, customer support, preventing abuse, and complying with law. We do not combine them with any other data about you, because we don't have any.

4.5 Managing, canceling and refunds

Android:

  • Manage or cancel:
    • in the app, use Manage subscription; or
    • in the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions; or
    • visit https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • When cancellation takes effect: under Google Play's general policy, it takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep Pro until then. Deleting the app or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds: refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies and applicable law. You may also contact us at the address in Section 11.

iPhone:

  • Manage or cancel:
    • in the app, open the Pro screen and tap Manage Subscription; or
    • open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions; or
    • in the App Store app, tap your account picture, then Subscriptions.
  • When cancellation takes effect: the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. If you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases are refunded by Apple, not by the developer. You can request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. You may also contact us at the address in Section 11.

4.6 Restoring Pro

  • Android: your subscription is linked to the Google account you used to buy it. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device and sign in to Google Play with the same account, the app restores Pro by checking with Google Play. You can also tap Restore purchases.
  • iPhone: your subscription is linked to the Apple Account you used to buy it. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling the app, sign in with the same Apple Account; the app checks with the App Store automatically, and you can tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen to sync your purchases with the App Store.

7. How data is protected

  • All network connections use HTTPS (TLS). Android: the app blocks unencrypted (cleartext) traffic and trusts only the device's system certificate authorities. iPhone: the app uses Apple's App Transport Security with no exceptions, so unencrypted connections are not allowed.
  • Saved data stays in the app's private storage, which Android and iOS isolate from other apps. Files are shared only through the system's share sheet or file picker, and only when you choose to.
  • The app requests the minimum permissions it needs: no precise or background location, and no broad storage or photo-library access.
  • Coordinates are rounded to the nearest 0.1° before any position-based weather request leaves your device.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. The app never handles your payment credentials, and it saves no purchase token, transaction ID or order ID. On iPhone, Pro is unlocked only by transactions that pass StoreKit's signature verification.
  • Android: the Play Billing Library sends its diagnostics to Google over HTTPS. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup, and the Pro status file is also excluded from device-to-device transfer.
  • iPhone: checklist logs, the custom checklist and the Pro status file are excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed to keep as little data as possible and to keep it on your device.
